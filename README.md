# Westminster Digital Transformation Readiness Diagnostic

An interactive group diagnostic tool developed for the University of Westminster Executive Workshop, 22 September 2026.

## What this is

A web-based diagnostic drawn from published best practice, sector case studies, and Jisc guidance on digital transformation in UK higher education. It is designed to help groups of senior colleagues identify what high-performing institutions have in place - and where gaps exist at their own institution.

The tool presents 12 statements across five themes - Strategy and planning, Governance, Data and systems, Staff capability, and Culture and change. Groups rate each statement as Yes (in place), Partial, or Not yet, and then work through three structured reflection questions to prepare for a group report-back.

## How to use it

Open the link below on any device - laptop, tablet, or phone. No installation or login required.

**Live tool:** https://saundeg.github.io/westminster-digital-diagnostic/diagnostic.html

Work through the 12 statements as a group. Once all statements are rated, the tool generates a summary showing where gaps cluster by theme. Groups then complete three reflection questions before reporting back to the wider group.

## Background

This tool was developed as part of the University of Westminster's response to the Jisc Digital Maturity Assessment completed in 2026, which placed the University at an overall mean of 2.46 out of 5 (Level 2 - Working towards Proactive) against the six Jisc thematic areas. The diagnostic was designed for use at an executive workshop on 22 September 2026, facilitated by Jisc, to help senior colleagues identify priorities for a Digital Transformation Roadmap.

## Themes covered

- Strategy and planning
- Governance
- Data and systems
- Staff capability
- Culture and change
